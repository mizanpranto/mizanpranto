## 👋 About Me

I started out as an **ethical hacker** — that's still where my head goes first. Work has since pulled me into security consulting, governance, and leadership, but the hands-on offensive mindset is what got me here and what I keep coming back to.

Today I'm an **AI & Cybersecurity Consultant** at **HSA Group**, leading a penetration testing team and advising on security architecture, threat modeling, and AI-driven vulnerability analysis. Before that, I ran enterprise-wide cybersecurity strategy as **Cybersecurity Lead & Program Director** at SMP CyberSecurity Ltd., delivering ISO/IEC 27001-aligned governance programs to government and private-sector clients, and trained 4,000+ learners in ethical hacking, network defense, and secure development.

Outside client work, I run an independent research pipeline studying **security vulnerabilities in GPT-generated code** — dataset construction, static analysis, and CWE classification, working toward publication.
